SAVORI GLOBAL SERIES NO. 01

Bangkok
Green Curry

PREP + COOK 25 MINS
SERVES 2 ADULTS
HEAT LEVEL MEDIUM (3/5)

A vibrant tug-of-war between creamy coconut, sharp galangal, and the citrus high notes of kaffir lime. This is street-side Bangkok in your kitchen.

Fresh Pantry

  • 2 Chicken Breasts (cubed)
  • 1 Can Coconut Milk (400ml)
  • 1 Small Eggplant
  • Handful of Green Beans
  • 1 tsp Fish Sauce (optional)
  • Cooking Oil

THE SAVORI SACHET

  • Fresh-ground Galangal & Lemongrass
  • Hero: Burlap & Barrel Turmeric
  • Toasted Coriander & Cumin
  • Dehydrated Kaffir Lime Leaf

Instructions

BLOOM: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium heat. Pour in the Savori sachet and stir for 45 seconds until the kitchen smells incredible.
SEAR: Add chicken and eggplant. Cook for 3-4 minutes until chicken is golden on the outside.
SIMMER: Pour in coconut milk and add green beans. Bring to a gentle simmer for 10-12 minutes until vegetables are tender.
FINISH: Stir in fish sauce or a pinch of salt. Serve over jasmine rice and garnish with fresh Thai basil or lime.
CHEF'S TIP: Don't let the coconut milk boil vigorously—a gentle simmer keeps the sauce velvety and prevents the spices from becoming bitter.
