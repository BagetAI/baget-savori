SAVORI GLOBAL SERIES NO. 03

Chengdu
Mapo Tofu

PREP + COOK 15 MINS
SERVES 2 ADULTS
HEAT LEVEL MALA HOT (4/5)

Achieve the legendary "Mala" (numbing and spicy) effect. Silky tofu meets the fermented depth and electric tingle of true Szechuan peppercorns.

Fresh Pantry

  • 1 Block Soft Tofu (cubed)
  • 100g Ground Pork or Mushrooms
  • 2 Green Onions (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • Chicken or Veg Stock (100ml)
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch

THE SAVORI SACHET

  • Fermented Bean Seasoning
  • Hero: Szechuan Peppercorns
  • Toasted Facing-Heaven Chilis
  • Garlic & Ginger Powder

Instructions

FRY: Brown the pork/mushrooms in a wok with oil. Add the Savori sachet and stir for 20 seconds until the oil turns red.
GLAZE: Add stock and soy sauce. Bring to a simmer. Gently slide in the tofu cubes so they don't break.
THICKEN: Mix cornstarch with a splash of water and stir into the sauce. Simmer for 3 minutes until glossy and thick.
TINGLE: Turn off heat. Sprinkle the reserved peppercorn dust (from the sachet top) over the dish. Garnish with green onions.
CHEF'S TIP: Don't stir with a spoon—gently swirl the wok or pan. This keeps the tofu silky and intact for a professional finish.
