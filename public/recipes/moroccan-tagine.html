SAVORI GLOBAL SERIES NO. 02

Marrakesh
Lamb Tagine

PREP + COOK 45 MINS
SERVES 2 ADULTS
HEAT LEVEL MILD & AROMATIC

Warm, aromatic, and deeply layered. A complex dance of cinnamon, ginger, and cardamom, finished with a whisper of saffron sweetness.

Fresh Pantry

  • 400g Lamb Shoulder (diced)
  • 1 Large Onion (diced)
  • 1 Sweet Potato (cubed)
  • Handful of Dried Apricots
  • Beef Stock (250ml)
  • Fresh Cilantro

THE SAVORI SACHET

  • Authentic Ras el Hanout
  • Hero: Grade-A Saffron Threads
  • Ceylon Cinnamon & Nutmeg
  • Smoked Paprika & Ginger

Instructions

BROWN: Heat oil in a heavy pot. Brown the lamb on all sides. Remove and set aside. Sauté onions in the same pot until soft.
TOAST: Add the Savori sachet (including the saffron envelope). Stir for 30 seconds to wake up the oils.
BRAISE: Return lamb to pot. Add sweet potato, apricots, and beef stock. Cover and simmer on low for 35-40 mins.
REST: Turn off heat and let sit for 5 minutes. The sauce will thicken into a rich glaze. Top with cilantro and serve with couscous.
CHEF'S TIP: For the most tender lamb, use a heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven. Slow and steady heat is the secret to Moroccan soul food.
